# Ingredients
- **15.5 ounce can garbanzo**
- **6 tbsp (1/4 + 1/8 cup) tahini**
- **3 tbsp fresh lemon**
- 1 clove garlic
- .25 cup EVOO
- .25 cup water
- .5 spoon salt, pepper
- spices - cumin, paprika, cayenne
# Instructions
- add everything except oil/water, pulse 10-12 times
- add oil/water gradually
