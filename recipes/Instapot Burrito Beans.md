# Ingredients
- **2 cups pinto dried**
- **1 yellow onion**
- **1 jalapeno**
- 3 cloves garlic
- 4 cups chicken stock
- 3 cups water
- 2 bay leaves
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp oregano
- cayenne
# Instructions
- rinse/strain beans, remove damaged, soak 8 hours? (overnight)
- instapot saute oil - when hot add onion/jalaapeno 2 min
- add garlic - 30 secc
- add a little stock and scrape, then add everything
- High for 45 min (consider 55 min), natural relase for 25 min, then vent
- reserve 2 cups bean liquid, drain rest, mash beans and add liquid as needed
