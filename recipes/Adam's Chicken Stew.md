# Ingredients
- **whole chicken**
- **zucchini**
- **1 head celery**
- **2 small onions**
- **half bag carrots (like 6?)**
- **parsnips**
- garlic
- turmeric
- seasonings - bay leaf, peppercorns, sage, rosemary, thyme, parsley
# Instructions
- put whole chicken, stock vegetables, cover with water and simmer for 1-2 hrs (longer for bone broth)
- drain chicken, fish it out, remove stock vegetables
- optionally strain, simmer egg to help
- add all vegetable (optionally time them), add salt, simmer
- pick out chicken meat (gloves), chop it up to make smaller fibers
- add chicken meat and simmer for half-hour more
- add spices
# Modifications (Bone Broth)
- Take off thighs at beginning
- add 1 tbsp of Apple Cider Vinegar at beginning
- at point of removing chicken, move over to instapot. Soup mode-High for 2 hrs
# Tips
- use gloves to pick chicken
- put original boiling pot into fridge or something? or clean it?
