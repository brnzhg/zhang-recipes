# Ingredients
- **1.75 lb chicken breast**
- **2 lemons**
- .25 cups mayo
- 1 tbsp mustard
- 1 tbsp parsley
- 1 tbsp chives
- .5 cup red onion
- .5 cup celery
- 1 clove garlic
- salt/pepper
## Mayo
- 1 egg
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 cup oil
# Instructions
## Mayo
- add everything but seasoning
- add oil after, settle 15 seconds
- blend bottom on high, **no pulse**, as mayo forms slowly tilt to get rest of oil
## Poaching
- 1.5 lb chicken, 8 cups water, 1.5 tbsp salt, stock veggies
- bring all to 150-155, keep there for 30min-1hr, cool in cold water right away
## Chicken Salad
- cube chicken, mix
