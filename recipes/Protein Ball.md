# Ingredients
## Choco PP
- 1.5 cups old fashioned rolled oats
- 1 cup PB or Almond Butter
- .25 cup sweetener
- 2 scoops (50-60g) choc protein powder
- 2 tbsp choc chips
## Tahini
- 2 cups rolled oats to 1 cup tahini
## Alternative (GimmeSomeOven)
- replace .5 cups with ground flax
- add vanilla
- add 1 tbsp chia seeds
# Instructions
- Mix all in bowl
- If it's too crumbly, add some water
