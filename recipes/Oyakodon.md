# Ingredients
- **12oz chicken (breast or thigh)**
- **1 large onion**
- 2 eggs + 2 whites
- 1 cup dashi (or chicken stock)
- 2 tbsp sake (or mirin for sugar)
- 1 tbsp soy
- togorashi seasoning
- scallions
# Instructions
- Bring dashi, soy, sake to a strong simmer
- Add onion (5 mins)
- Add chicken pieces (~4 mins for breast, ~5-7 thighs, should evap half)
- Reduce to low simmer, add beaten eggs (lid)
