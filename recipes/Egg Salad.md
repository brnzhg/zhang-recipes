# Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- **chopped dill**
- **3/4 cup celery**
- **red onion**
- **radish**
- 1/4 cup greek yogurt
- 1 tsp dijon
- salt/pepper

# Instructions
- boil egg, keep lid on and turn off heat for 10 min, then dunk in cold
- mix everything, use potato masher or gloves
