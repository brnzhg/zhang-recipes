# Ingredients
- **12 eggs**
- 1/2 tbsp black tea leaves
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/8 cup sugar
- 1/8 cup salt
- **ginger root**
- 2 bay leaves
- 3 star anise
- 1 stick cinnamon
- fennel
# Instructions
- Boil the eggs; once the water reaches a boil, cook for 7-8 minutes.
- Take the eggs out and immerse in cold water.
- Crack the shells.
- Put the eggs back in the pot and add all the spices.
- Pour in enough water to cover the eggs and cook on medium heat for 30 minutes or more.
