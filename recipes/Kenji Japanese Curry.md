# Ingredients
- **1 pound chicken**
- **.5 onion**
- **1 large potato (try turnips)**
- **1 large carrot**
- **frozen peas**
- 3-4 cloves garlic
- ginger
- 2-3 tbsp curry
- 4 tbsps flour
- 4 tbsp butter/oil
- 4 cups broth, can dilute (or less?)
- add tumeric
# Instructions
- medium high brown chicken 3 mins
- add onions, salt pepper , soften 3 mins
- add garlic ginger - 30 seconds
- add curry powder - 30 seconds
- add flour, stir remove pockets, toast - 30 secs
- add broth/water to dissolve flour, add veg, optional honey
- cover and cook - 10 mins
- add  in peas
