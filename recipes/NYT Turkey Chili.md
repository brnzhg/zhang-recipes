# Ingredients
- **2 lbs ground turkey or beef**
- **1 onion**
- **1 red pepper**
- **1 cup celery**
- **1 jalapeno**
- **24 oz diced tomato**
- **2x 15 ounce red kidney beans**
- 2 tbsp garlic
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 tbsp oregano
- 3 tbsp chili pwder
- 2 tsp cumin
- 2 cup chicken broth
- .25 cup Worcestershire
- basil

# Instructions
- heat oil, add turkey, cook for 5 mins chopping and browning
- add all veg and spices, cook 5 mins
- add tomatos, chicken broth, salt/pepper
- boil, then simmer for 15 mins
- add beans, cook 10 more mins
