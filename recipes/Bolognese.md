# Ingredients
- 2.2 lbs ground beef (really 50/50 pork/beef)
- 1L (4.2 cups) tomato puree (passata)
- 14.1 oz peeled tomoatoes
- 5.3 oz tomato paste
- 1 glass red wine (big cup)
- 1 mug whole milk
- 1 red onion
- 3 celery stalks
- 3 carrots
# Instructions
- fry onion until golden
- add carrots/celery (4-5 mins)
- add half glass wine, simmer low until gone
- add mince, break before season, brown all around
- when dry, add rest of wine
- add all tomatoes, crush them, add water to clear out tomato
- LOW simmer, total 4-5 hrs adding water when dry
- add cream at end, simmer 4-5 more mins
# Tips
- fry mince concurrently in separate pan, get all water out
- wine simmer in mince and veg separately for speed
