# Ingredients
- **4 cloves garlic, 1 knob ginger**
- **1-6 thai chillies**
- **1 onion**
- **14 oz whole peeeled tomato**
- **2 14 oz chickpeas**
- **2 tbsp lemon juice**
- 2 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 tsp cumin seed
- 2 tsp ground coriander
- .5 tsp tumeric
- 1.5 tsp garam masala
- try adding dried mango powder, kasuri methi, red chili powder
