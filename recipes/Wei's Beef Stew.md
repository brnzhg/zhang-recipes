# Ingredients
- **1 pound chuck beef**
- **1 sweet onion**
- **1/4 head small red cabbage**
- **2 carrots**
- **4 red potatoes**
- **14-ounce can tomato sauce**
- chicken stock
# Instructions
- In a skillet, use a little oil to brown the beef.
- immerse the beef with chicken stock and water, simmer for 1/2 hour.
- sauté onions, then put carrots and red cabbage in, sauté for several minutes.
- add veg and potatoes to pot, add tomato sauce, add stock/water
- boil, then reduce heat to low and simmer 1 hour, add salt and pepper to taste.
