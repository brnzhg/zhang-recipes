# Ingredients
- 3/4 cup gluten-free rolled oats
- 1 cup unsweetened soy milk
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 2 tbsp hemp hearts or flax seeds
- **1/2 ripe banana**
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 tbsp natural peanut butter or almond butter (optional)
- **1/2 cup fresh or frozen berries**
- small handful walnuts or pumpkin seeds
# Instructions
- Mash the banana in a mason jar or a container with a lid until mostly smooth.
- Add the oats, chia seeds, hemp hearts, cinnamon, and vanilla extract.
- Pour in the soy milk, add the peanut butter if using, and stir vigorously for about 30 seconds so the chia seeds don't clump.
- Cover and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours).
- In the morning, stir well; splash in a little more soy milk if too thick. Top with the berries and walnuts or pumpkin seeds.
# Meal Prep Tip
- Make 3 or 4 jars at a time on Sunday night; they keep in the fridge for up to 5 days.
