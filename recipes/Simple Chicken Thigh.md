# Instructions
- For safety prepare to 165, preferrable to 175
- Dry brine for 1 hr in fridge
- Sear on pan, then oven 350
- Alternatively, sear on pan, then cover lid
# Tips
- Pad dry with paper towel to sear (dry brine helps?)
