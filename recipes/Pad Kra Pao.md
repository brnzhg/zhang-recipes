# Ingredients
- **1 lb ground meat**
- **3 Thai chilis**
- **.5 cup Mild/sweet red chilis (mini, long red, red anaheim)**
- 6 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp sugar
- .5 cup chicken stock
- 1.25 cup holy basil
- 1-2 shallots (red onion?)
- 2/3 cup green beans
- fried eggs
## Sauce
- 1-2 Thai chilies
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 2 tsp lime juice
- 1 clove garlic
# Instructions
- paste with 3 thai chilies, half red peppers, garlic
- maybe fry shallot, green beans and red peppers seperately - 3-4 mins?
- fry beef until sizzling in fat
- turn down heat, push beef to side, add paste until golden then toss with beef
- maybe mix veg in now?
- high, add half stock, fish sauce, sugar, black pepper. Add rest of red peppers - 30 sec
- turn off heat, add basil, mix until wilted, add water if too dry
