# Ingredients
- **.5 cup yellow onion**
- **1 lb ground beef**
- **2 cup diced sweet potato**
- **14.5 oz fire roasted diced tomatos**
- **4 oz canned green chiles**
- 5 tsp chili powder
- 2 tsp cumin
- garlic
- cilantro
- .25 cup water
# Instructions
- cook beef and onion (really?), add some chili powder and cumin, salt and pepper
- when beef coked add garlic cook 1min
- remove beef, add sweet potato, tomatos, chilies, more chili powder, more cumin, salt and pepper
- stir, lid, simmer 20 mins
- remix, add cheese
