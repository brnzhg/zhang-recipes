# Ingredients
- **1 cup toor dal (split yellow pigeon peas), for red lentils do 3 min insta** - I use 2 cups Moong dal and do 15-20mins with 4 cups water
- **1 small onion**
- **2 serrano chili**
- **1 romato tomato**
- 2 tsp ghee
- 1 tsp black mustard seeds
- 3 cloves garlic, 1 inch ginger
- 1 tsp salt (put at end?)
- .5 tsp tumeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- .5 tsp red chili powder
## Tadka
- **1 red dry chili, Indian or Arbol**
- 2 tsp ghee
- .5 tsp cumin seeds
- .5 tsp mustard seeds
- 1 pinch hing (asafetida)
- .25 tsp red chili powder
- 6 curry leaves

# Instructions
- rinse and soak lentils
- instapot hot, add oil and mustard seeds until splutter
- add onions, serrano, ginger garlic until onions pink
- add tomato, spices, saute for min (bloom)
- add lentils with 2 cups water to 1 cup lentils, scrape bottom - pressure cook for 6 mins high
- 5-10 mins natural release, then manual
## Tadka
- ghee to small sauce pan with dry red chili, cumin seeds, mustard seeds, hing, red chili powder, curry leaves
- gently heat until splutter, add to dal before serving
