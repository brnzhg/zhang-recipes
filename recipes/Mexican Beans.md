# Ingredients
- **1 lb dried pinto beans**
- **2 (10 oz) cans diced tomatos with green chile**
- **.5 lb bacon**
- **1 yellow onion**
- 1 tbsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp cumin
- 1.5 tsp garlic powder
- cilantro
- bay leaf
- salt
# Instructions
- Optionally soak overnight
- Rinse beans, remove any floaters
- 4-5 cups water in instapot (enough cover by 2-3 in)
- Add everything, 30 min
- *Consider frying bacon first, adding tomatoes after beans are tender*
- Natural release 15 min
