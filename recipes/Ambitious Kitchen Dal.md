# Ingredients
- **1 cup yellow moong dal/red split**
- **1 onion**
- **15 oz crushed tomatoes**
- **13.5 oz coconut milk (reduced fat)**
- **1 tbsp lemon juice**
- ginger garlic - 5 cloves, 2-inch ginger
- 1 tsp tomato paste
- 2 cups vegetable broth
- .5 tsp tumeric
- 1.5 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp coriander
- .5 tsp cayanne

# Instructions
- wash lentils, soak 30 mins
- saute onion 3-4 mins, clear
- add ginger/garlic - 2-3 mins, brown
- add tumeric, cumin, coriander, cayenne - saute masala 2-3 mins
- add tomato paste 1-2 mins, pour tomatoes 3-4 mins
- add coconut milk and broth, simmer 5 mins
- add lentils, simmer for 30 mins (need 30 mins boil to destroy Lectin)
