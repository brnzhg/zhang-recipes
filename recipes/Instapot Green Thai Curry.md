# Ingredients
- **2 tbsp green thai curry paste**
- **4lb chicken**
- **14oz coconut milk**
- **1 eggplant (4 cups)**
- **3 thai chiles**
- **kabocha squash / butternut / acorn**
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 in ginger
- 1/4 tsp coriander
- 1/8 tsp cumin
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- .5 cup cilantro
- .5 cup thai basil
- 1 spoon of salt?

# Instructions
- fry garlic, ginger, chiles (chopped), coriander, cumin, curry paste - 1 min
- add curry paste - fry 3 min
- add coconut milk - 1 min
- season chicken
- layer eggplant, chicken, eggplant, instapot 20mins
	- consider adding eggplant at end?
