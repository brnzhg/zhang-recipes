# Ingredients
- **3 lb chuck roast**
- **1.5 lb little potatoes**
- **3 carrots**
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp parsley
- .5 tsp onion pwder
- 1 tbsp worcestire
- 2 tbsp balsamic
# Instructions
- instant pot saute, wait til reads "hot" sear 2-3 minutes per side
- add Broth, Tomato paste, vinegar, worcetshire sauce - scrape
- manual/pressure cook, 65 minutes
- turn off and natural release 10 mins
- add potatos and carrots, manual/pressure cook 3 minutes
- natural 10 mins
